Blocks | English release for GitHub Pages

Unzip the archive and upload its contents to the repository root. Keep index.html, assets/, .nojekyll, and THREE-LICENSE.txt together.
In GitHub, go to Settings > Pages > Deploy from a branch. Select your publishing branch and / (root), then save.
The site works at the domain root and under a repository path. It needs no build step or backend.

The 3D viewer loads separately after the page first renders. Gallery models load as you approach the gallery, and the Full HD video loads when you press Play. The build demo shows the editor with its interface and step labels in English.
To preview locally, serve the extracted folder over HTTP and open it in your browser.
